About us

Welcome to ZPUPSA Gadiwat School

ZPUPSA GADIWAT was established in 1960 and it is managed by the Local body. It is located in Rural area. It is located in AURANGABAD block of AURANGABAD (MAHARASHTRA) district of Maharashtra. The school consists of Grades from 1 to 8. The school is Co-educational and it doesn't have an attached pre-primary section. The school is N/A in nature and is not using school building as a shift-school. Marathi is the medium of instructions in this school. This school is approachable by all weather road.
